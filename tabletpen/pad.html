Remote mode (🖱)

  • 1 finger moves the pointer · tap = click · two taps = double-click
  • 2-finger tap = right-click · 3-finger tap = middle-click
  • 2 fingers slide to scroll
  • Press & hold still (blue border) = grab, then slide to drag, lift to drop
  • ⌨ Type opens the keyboard. Tap anywhere outside it to close it.
  • The pen still draws while remote mode is on.

Pen & finger

  • Pen and finger draw on the PC in any app (Paint, OneNote, Whiteboard, Krita…), the pen with pressure and tilt.
  • 📸 shot saves a full-resolution screenshot in the PC's Pictures › Screenshots folder.

Paint (🎨)

  • Opens Windows Paint on the PC and shows its canvas here. Tools, shapes, colors and size on the bar act in Paint live.
  • Draw with the pen (pressure works) or a finger. 🗑️ clears the canvas, 💾 saves.

YouTube

  • YouTube runs right on the tablet (smooth, instant), muted – the PC plays the same video in sync, so the sound comes from the PC. Fullscreen and the back button work. Tap the tab again to go back.
  • Signed in: use Sign in to YouTube (for the tablet) in the PC's tray menu once; the tablet then uses that account.
  • ☰ keys (bottom of the sidebar) shows or hides the key bar – its volume keys control the PC.
